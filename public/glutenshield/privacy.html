Privacy Policy

GlutenShield

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 · Version 1.0

GlutenShield (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is built to empower individuals with celiac disease, wheat allergies, and gluten sensitivities to navigate dining and grocery shopping safely. We respect your privacy and design our software around a privacy-first, on-device architecture.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

📸 Camera (Label & Physical Menu OCR)

GlutenShield requests access to your device camera exclusively to allow you to photograph food ingredient labels, barcodes, and restaurant menus. All Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed locally on your device using Google ML Kit. Your photos are analyzed in volatile memory and are not uploaded, stored on external cloud servers, or shared with third parties.

📍 Location Services (Nearby Safe Dining)

When you use the dining locator or “Nearby” feature, GlutenShield requests approximate or precise location solely to display celiac-verified eateries near your current location. Location coordinates are never logged, tracked, stored, or tied to your personal identity.

💾 On-Device Storage

Your saved scan history, favorite safe dining spots, kitchen audit notes, and custom allergen preferences are stored locally in private application sandbox storage on your device. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

GlutenShield may integrate Google AdMob to display banner or interstitial advertisements to support ongoing development. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and crash diagnostics according to Google’s Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

3. Children’s Privacy

GlutenShield is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security safeguards. Because the core OCR, barcode lookup cache, and allergen audit rules operate locally on your device, your private dietary scans remain securely on your phone.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
